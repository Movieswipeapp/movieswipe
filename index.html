Stop scrolling. Swipe, match, watch.

MovieSwipe is an iOS app that helps you decide what to watch. You swipe through films the way you would on a dating app — right for yes, left for no. Swipe alone, or start a shared session so you and your partner or friends swipe at the same time and only see the films everyone said yes to. An AI search lets you describe what you're in the mood for in your own words instead of scrolling through genre filters.

The problem MovieSwipe solves

Most people do not have a shortage of films to watch. They have a decision problem. A typical streaming home screen shows more than two hundred titles at once, and looking at two hundred options is harder than answering two hundred yes-or-no questions. Add a second person with different taste and the evening turns into a negotiation. Surveys of streaming users repeatedly find that people spend somewhere between fifteen and thirty minutes browsing before they start watching — and a meaningful share give up and watch nothing.

MovieSwipe replaces that browsing with a sequence of binary choices. One film at a time, one decision at a time, roughly a second per title. A session usually takes under a minute.

How it works

  1. Open the app and start swiping. You see one film at a time: poster, title, year, a short synopsis. Swipe right if you'd watch it, left if you wouldn't.
  2. Invite someone, or don't. Swiping solo builds your watch queue for later. Starting a shared session lets other people swipe on the same set of films at the same time, on their own phone.
  3. Watch the match appear. As soon as everyone in the session swipes right on the same film, it surfaces as a match. That's your evening decided.

What MovieSwipe does

  • Shared swipe sessions. Two or more people swipe at the same time. You only see the films everyone agreed on, so nobody has to argue for their pick.
  • AI search in plain language. Describe what you want the way you'd say it out loud — "a comedy like Palm Springs but shorter", "something like Arrival without the sad ending" — and get matching films back. No genre dropdowns, no filter stacking.
  • Find films similar to ones you love. Name a film you already like and MovieSwipe builds a swipe deck around it.
  • A personal queue. Every right swipe goes into a list you can come back to, so a swipe session is never wasted.
  • Apple Vision Pro. MovieSwipe runs natively on visionOS 2.2 and later, not just as a compatibility build.

Who it's for

MovieSwipe is built for the moment before the film starts, not for cataloguing what you've already seen. It's most useful for couples who watch together several times a week, for friend groups deciding on a movie night, for families who need a pick everyone can live with, and for anyone who opens a streaming app, scrolls for twenty minutes and closes it again.

Pricing

MovieSwipe is free to download and free to use for solo swiping. MovieSwipe Pro unlocks unlimited AI searches and shared sessions, and costs €2.99 per week or €6.99 per month. Both are auto-renewing subscriptions you can cancel at any time in your Apple account settings. There is no ad-supported tier and no data resale.

Availability

MovieSwipe requires iOS or iPadOS 18.2 or later, or visionOS 2.2 or later on Apple Vision Pro. There is no Android or web version. The interface is available in English, Dutch, German, French, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.
