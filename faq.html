Frequently asked questions

Everything people ask about MovieSwipe — pricing, platforms, shared sessions and what the app deliberately doesn't do.

What is MovieSwipe?

MovieSwipe is an iOS and Apple Vision Pro app that helps you decide what to watch. You swipe right on films you want to see and left on the ones you don't. In a shared session, two or more people swipe at the same time and the app surfaces only the films everyone said yes to.

Is MovieSwipe free?

MovieSwipe is free to download and free to use for solo swiping. MovieSwipe Pro unlocks unlimited AI searches and shared sessions and costs €2.99 per week or €6.99 per month.

How much does MovieSwipe Pro cost?

€2.99 per week or €6.99 per month, as an auto-renewing subscription. If you use the app more than a couple of times a week the monthly plan is cheaper — the weekly plan works out to roughly €13 per month. Prices may differ slightly by country.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, select MovieSwipe and tap Cancel Subscription. The subscription stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for. Cancelling is handled entirely by Apple; we cannot cancel it for you.

Is MovieSwipe available on Android?

No. MovieSwipe is available for iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro only. There is no Android version and no web version. If you and the person you watch with are on different platforms, a cross-platform app such as Matched will suit you better.

Which iOS version do I need?

iOS or iPadOS 18.2 or later on iPhone and iPad, or visionOS 2.2 or later on Apple Vision Pro.

Does MovieSwipe work on Apple Vision Pro?

Yes. MovieSwipe is a native visionOS app, not an iPad compatibility build. It requires visionOS 2.2 or later.

How many people can swipe together?

A shared session supports more than two people, so it works for couples, friend groups and families. The more people in a session, the longer it takes to reach a film everyone accepts.

Does everyone need to be in the same room?

No. Each person swipes on their own phone, so shared sessions work for long-distance couples and for friends in different places watching the same film at the same time.

How does the AI search work?

You describe what you're in the mood for in ordinary language — for example "a comedy like Palm Springs but shorter" or "something tense but not violent" — and MovieSwipe builds a swipe deck around that description instead of asking you to set genre, decade and rating filters.

Can I find films similar to one I already like?

Yes. Name a film you enjoyed and MovieSwipe builds a deck of films around it, which you then swipe through as normal.

Does MovieSwipe tell me where a film is streaming?

Streaming availability is limited in MovieSwipe. If your main question is which service a specific film is on, a dedicated availability app such as JustWatch or Reelgood will answer it better. MovieSwipe is built for the deciding step, not the finding step.

Does MovieSwipe track what I've already watched?

MovieSwipe keeps a personal queue of films you've swiped right on, but it is not a full watch-history tracker. For logging, rating and reviewing everything you watch, Letterboxd or Trakt are the right tools.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. MovieSwipe loads film data and runs shared sessions over the internet, so an active connection is required.

What happens to films I swipe right on that don't match?

They go into your personal queue, so a session that ends without a match still leaves you with a shortlist to come back to.

In which countries is MovieSwipe available?

MovieSwipe is available worldwide on the App Store. The interface is localised in English, Dutch, German, French, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.

What data does MovieSwipe collect?

See the privacy policy for the full and current answer. MovieSwipe does not sell personal data.

Is there a free trial of Pro?

Trial availability is shown on the subscription screen inside the app and on the App Store listing, since it can change by region and over time.

Can I use MovieSwipe on my own, without anyone else?

Yes. Solo swiping is free and builds your personal queue. Shared sessions are the reason most people install it, but they're optional.
