Looking for a Matched alternative?

Matched is the best-known couples movie app, and for a lot of people it's the right one. If you're looking for an alternative, here's a straight comparison with MovieSwipe — including the cases where you should stay with Matched.

The one-line difference

Both apps let two people swipe on films separately and surface the ones you both said yes to. MovieSwipe adds a plain-language search that lets you shape the deck before you start swiping, and runs natively on Apple Vision Pro. Matched is on both iOS and Android, which MovieSwipe is not.

Stay with Matched if…

  • One of you is on Android. This is decisive. MovieSwipe is iOS and visionOS only, so a mixed-platform couple can't use it at all.
  • You want the simplest possible thing. Matched does one job with very little surface area. If extra features read as clutter to you, that's a real advantage.
  • Track record matters to you. Matched has been around longer and has more reviews and press behind it. MovieSwipe is newer and has a small review count — that's a fair thing to weigh.

Switch to MovieSwipe if…

  • You keep swiping past films that were never going to work. Being able to say "something funny, under 100 minutes, nothing about weddings" and rebuild the deck around it saves the part of the session that actually gets tedious.
  • You think in terms of films you already love. Naming one film and getting a deck of neighbours around it is a different starting point from a general catalogue.
  • You have a Vision Pro. MovieSwipe is a native visionOS app, not a compatibility build.
  • You don't watch in English. The interface ships in Dutch, German, French, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese as well as English.

Pricing

MovieSwipe is free to download. Solo swiping is free. MovieSwipe Pro unlocks unlimited shared sessions and AI searches for €2.99 per week or €6.99 per month, cancellable at any time in your Apple account settings. If you use it more than a couple of times a week, the monthly plan is the cheaper option — the weekly plan works out to roughly €13 a month.

Trying both

Both apps are free to download, so the honest advice is to install both, run one session each on the same evening, and keep whichever produced a film faster. That takes ten minutes and tells you more than any comparison table.
